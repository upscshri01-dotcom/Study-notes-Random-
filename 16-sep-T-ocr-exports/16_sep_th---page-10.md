# Cong. alleges AAP-GFP tie-up aims at helping BJP in Goa, AAP in Punjab

Press Trust of India
PANAJI

The Congress on Tuesday accused the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP)-Goa Forward Party (GFP) alliance of being an "exchange offer" with the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), alleging that the arrangement was aimed at helping the BJP retain power in Goa and AAP in Punjab by supporting each other in the coming elections.

AAP and the Vijai Sardesai-led GFP recently announced the "Goa First" alliance ahead of the 2027 Assembly election, with a focus on "defections-free" politics.

Goa Pradesh Congress Committee president Girish Chodankar alleged, "The 'Goa First' alliance had gone against the aspirations of the people of Goa, who wanted a larger opposition alliance to take on the BJP in the 2027 elections. AAP must have made an exchange offer ensuring that the BJP will help AAP win Punjab elections, while in Goa, AAP will support BJP."

Mr. Chodankar claimed that the alliance was being formed to serve the BJP's interests rather than those of the people of Goa.

"People of Goa are opposed to this unholy alliance and would reject these parties in the polls. They won't be able to win even a single seat. In Goa, it would be 'Goa Last' for them," he said.

In the 2022 Goa Assembly election, the BJP emerged as the single largest party, winning 20 seats in the 40-member House. The Congress won 11 seats, AAP two, the GFP one, the MGP two and the RGP one, while Independents won three. In September 2022, eight Congress MLAs, led by former Chief Minister Digambar Kamat, defected to the BJP, taking its strength in the Assembly to 28.

# AIUDF chief to contest Nagaon Lok Sabha bypoll

The Hindu Bureau
GUWAHATI

The All India United Democratic Front (AIUDF) on Tuesday named party president Mohammed Badruddin Ajmal as its candidate for the October 6 byelection to the Nagaon Lok Sabha constituency in Assam.

Mr. Ajmal will face former bureaucrat Devasish Sharma of the BJP and former MLA Sibamoni Bora of Congress as his prime rivals. The Trinamool has fielded Abdul Salam.
