# Punjab Cong. protests against AAP Minister over villager’s ‘death’

Congress leaders Sachin Pilot, Charanjit Singh Channi, Amrinder Singh Raja Warring and others during a protest in Chandigarh on Tuesday. PTI

The Hindu Bureau
CHANDIGARH

Punjab Congress workers on Tuesday held a demonstration here against Finance Minister Harpal Singh Cheema over the death of 47-year-old Gulzar Singh on September 7, even as the police resorted to water cannons to disperse the protesters.

Party leaders and workers assembled at the party office here and tried to breach the barricades put up by the administration on the way to Mr. Cheema’s official residence.

Punjab affairs general secretary in-charge Sachin Pilot led the demonstration, demanding the resignation of Mr. Cheema and an independent probe into the incident. “The Congress has nothing personal against him, but doesn’t the law demand registration of an FIR followed by a detailed probe when a minister’s name in his dying declaration,” he said.

The Opposition has been alleging that Singh was pressured by the local Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leaders to apologise for questioning Mr. Cheema over drug menace in his village during an event at Dirba in Sangrur district on September 6.

The police registered a case under relevant sections of the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act and booked five persons under Section 108 (abetment to suicide) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.

Mr. Pilot said Singh’s death was reflective of what was happening in Punjab against the claims made by AAP. “The situation is such that a poor Dalit had to pay with his life just because he raised a question about the availability of drugs to which his son had fallen a victim,” he alleged.

Mr. Pilot, State Congress chief Amrinder Singh Raja Warring and others were detained by police during the protest.

# Raut claims conspiracy in Disha Salian case; CM appeals not to speculate

Press Trust of India
MUMBAI

Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Sanjay Raut on Tuesday alleged that the Central Bureau of Investigation’s (CBI) FIR in the Disha Salian death case is a “political conspiracy orchestrated by the Centre to tarnish the Thackeray family’s reputation”.

Speaking to reporters, he said Mumbai police had already probed the matter thoroughly and cleared Aaditya Thackeray of any link to the former celebrity manager’s death in June 2020.

The CBI has registered an FIR in connection with the death of Salian, former manager of late actor Sushant Singh Rajput, naming no accused in compliance with the Bombay High Court directives on a petition filed by her father.

He said the CBI probe is being done to target Mr. Aaditya, who has been attacking the Devendra Fadnavis-led Maharashtra government on various public issues.

He also termed it an attempt to divert attention from Maratha quota activist Manoj Jarange-Patil’s agitation or put pressure on the Shiv Sena (UBT) to support the delimitation Bill in Parliament.

Meanwhile, Mr. Fadnavis said the CBI will investigate the case, and that it is “improper to engage in any kind of speculation” in this matter.

“We should see what the CBI investigates,” he said.
