# INBRIEF

**Former Andhra Pradesh Deputy CM Alla Nani dies**

YSRCP leader and former Andhra Pradesh Deputy Chief Minister Alla Kali Krishna Srinivas (popularly known as Nani) died of heart attack on Tuesday. He was 56. A three-time MLA from Eluru, Mr. Nani first won the seat in 2004. He also held the Health portfolio during the YSRCP government’s term. Leaders from across the political spectrum condoled his sudden demise. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu described his demise at a young age as heartbreaking.

**Four of a family killed in fire accident in Belagavi**

Four members of a family were killed on Monday after fire engulfed their house at Chavatgalli in Belagavi district of Karnataka. Two others injured in the incident were hospitalised. The police suspect that a refrigerator caught fire due to high voltage. The blaze then spread to other parts of the house. All the family members were caught inside the tin-shed structure, the police said. Senior officers inspected the house.

**Three-member gang held for temple theft in A.P., Karnataka**

The Andhra Pradesh Police have arrested three persons in connection with thefts in temples across the State and Karnataka. Around 21 kilograms of silver ornaments, melted silver lumps, and a two-wheeler, all worth nearly ₹40 lakh, were seized from the accused. Addressing mediapersons in Anantapur on Tuesday, Superintendent of Police P. Jagadeesh said the accused wore masks and gloves, and sprayed paint on CCTV cameras while committing thefts at the temples.

# Will fight ED’s attempt to implicate Pinarayi: CPI(M)

Party moves resolution condemning ‘political witch-hunt’ against Opposition leader and family, even as State police chief forwards ED letter seeking registration of case to govt. for further action

The Hindu Bureau
KOZHIKODE

The three-day extended state committee meeting of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) on Tuesday condemned the Enforcement Directorate’s (ED) “bid to implicate” Leader of the Opposition in the Keralam Assembly and Polit Bureau member Pinarayi Vijayan, his daughter, T. Veena, and her husband and party secretariat member P.A. Mohamed Riyas in the CMRL-Exalogic pay-off case.

The party response came on a day State Police Chief Ravada A. Chandrasekhar said that the ED letter recommending the registration of a case against Mr. Vijayan and others on corruption allegations, had been forwarded to the State government for further action.

“I received the letter from the ED. We have sent it to the government. We are waiting for necessary directives. The ED requested us to file an FIR, but we are still examining the matter,” he told mediapersons.

The CPI(M), in a resolution passed on the concluding day of the meeting, said the ED’s “political witch-hunt” “will be strongly resisted both legally and politically”.

‘All taxes paid’
The resolution pointed out that [the now defunct] Exalogic Solutions Private Ltd., a company owned Ms. Veena, and the CMRL engaged in a business transaction based on a legal agreement.

During the period, the applicable income tax and Goods and Services Tax were paid, and the company received remuneration through bank accounts.

Press Trust of India
HOSHIARPUR (PUNJAB)

A statue of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was allegedly vandalised in Barian Kalan village here on Tuesday, police said. Hoshiarpur SP (Investigation) Navneet Singh Mahal said the statue’s raised-hand finger was damaged when some unidentified persons allegedly threw stones at it.

# CPI(M) initiates ‘corrective’ steps in Keralam with secretariat reshuffle

The Hindu Bureau
KOZHIKODE

Significant changes have been brought in the State leadership of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) in Keralam as its three-day extended State committee meeting concluded here on Tuesday.

P. Jayarajan (Kannur), V. Sivankutty (Thiruvananthapuram), and M.B. Rajesh (Palakkad) will now be members of the State secretariat. John Brittas, special invitee to the Central Committee (CC), too will be part of it.

Nine leaders will be included in the State committee as permanent invitees. They are N. Sukanya (Kannur), O.S. Ambika (Thiruvananthapuram), Subaida Ishaq (Palakkad), Students Federation of India national president Adarsh M. Saji, Democratic Youth Federation of India (DYFI) State secretary M. Vijin, DYFI national functionary Jaick C. Thomas, SFI State president M. Sivaprasad and State secretary P.S. Sanjeev, and poet Prabha Varma.

Thomas Isaac out
CC members T.P. Ramakrishnan and T.M. Thomas Isaac have been removed from the secretariat though they can attend its meetings.

According to party sources, the changes are part of the corrective steps intended by the State committee meeting. These steps followed intensive discussions on a draft document presented by party State secretary M.V. Govindan that suggested guidelines for the revival of the party’s fortunes after the Assembly poll debacle.

He claimed there was no “restructuring of the leadership” as such, but it could be called “introduction of fresh faces”.
